How can we help?

Questions, feedback, and bug reports are welcome.

Contact support

Email the developer and include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened.

Email support

Restore Metronomeo Pro

Open Settings in Metronomeo and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles billing, renewal, and cancellation.

iCloud library sync

Sign in to iCloud on each device and enable iCloud Drive. In Metronomeo, open Settings, then iCloud & Backups. Your library is stored in your private iCloud account.

Ableton Link, MIDI, keyboards, and pedals

Both devices should be on the same local network for Ableton Link. MIDI and external controls can be configured from Settings. Connection features require Metronomeo Pro.

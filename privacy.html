metronomeo

Privacy, in plain language.

Your music stays private. Usage analytics are optional. No advertising or cross-app tracking.

Effective September 28, 2026

Information Metronomeo stores

Your settings, songs, playlists, and practice plans are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your library is also stored in your private iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. The developer does not operate a server that receives this library.

Purchases

Apple processes Metronomeo Pro subscriptions. Metronomeo receives verified purchase status from Apple to determine whether Pro features are available. The developer does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password.

Device access and connections

  • Local network access is used only when you enable Ableton Link or compatible network control.
  • MIDI, keyboard, Bluetooth pedal, and other external input events are used only to control the app.
  • Flashlight access is used only when you enable the optional beat light.
  • Haptics and screen pulses are generated on the device and are not collected.

Optional usage analytics

Only if you choose to share analytics, ifnoon sends feature usage, settings choices (such as tempo, meter, sound and palette), session duration, basic app/build/OS information, device category (iPhone or iPad), purchase outcomes and limited audio-error categories to PostHog and Mixpanel. Apple processes payments; analytics do not include receipts, transaction identifiers, card information or Apple Account details.

A random installation ID links sessions within Metronomeo. This is pseudonymous data, not a promise of complete anonymity. We do not use advertising identifiers, hardware fingerprints, personal names, email addresses, location enrichment, song or playlist names, MIDI messages, microphone audio, screen recordings or cross-app identity linking. Network providers necessarily receive an IP address to deliver requests; we disable analytics geolocation enrichment. Analytics are kept separate from other ifnoon apps within shared service accounts, and copied to our access-controlled PostgreSQL analytics backup.

We use these data only to understand feature adoption, improve usability and reliability, and measure subscription flows. PostHog and Mixpanel process analytics for these purposes under their applicable data-protection terms. Metronomeo does not show advertising, sell personal information, build advertising profiles, or track you across other apps and websites. All app functionality is available on the same terms whether you share analytics or not.

Retention and deletion

On-device library data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Data synchronized through iCloud is controlled through your Apple Account and iCloud settings. You can turn off library sync in Metronomeo settings.

In Settings → Privacy, turn off “Share usage analytics” to stop collection, cancel pending uploads and discard unsent events. Previously transmitted events are retained in our analytics services and database backup until deleted; turning off collection does not automatically delete that history. To request deletion, use the contact link and analytics ID shown on that screen. We use the ID to locate records in both providers and our backup. An unsent local queue is limited to 200 events and seven days. Deletion from backups must also be handled when a backup is restored.

Children

Metronomeo is a general-audience music utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.

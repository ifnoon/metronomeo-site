Privacy, in plain language.

Metronomeo does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

Effective September 22, 2026

Information Metronomeo stores

Your settings, songs, playlists, and practice plans are stored on your device. If you enable iCloud sync, your library is also stored in your private iCloud account so it can sync across your devices. The developer does not operate a server that receives this library.

Purchases

Apple processes Metronomeo Pro subscriptions. Metronomeo receives verified purchase status from Apple to determine whether Pro features are available. The developer does not receive your payment card number or Apple Account password.

Device access and connections

  • Local network access is used only when you enable Ableton Link or compatible network control.
  • MIDI, keyboard, Bluetooth pedal, and other external input events are used only to control the app.
  • Flashlight access is used only when you enable the optional beat light.
  • Haptics and screen pulses are generated on the device and are not collected.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Metronomeo does not use third-party analytics SDKs, show advertising, build advertising profiles, sell personal information, or track you across apps and websites.

Retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete it in the app or remove the app. Data synchronized through iCloud is controlled through your Apple Account and iCloud settings. You can turn off library sync in Metronomeo settings.

Children

Metronomeo is a general-audience music utility. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be posted on this page.

Contact
